Eigenfaces
English narration / synthetic voice

Recognising familiar faces.
Can a compact mathematical representation recognise familiar people? I adapted an Eigenfaces workflow to photographs of people I know, connecting linear algebra with a practical recognition task.

From pixels to principal components.
The workflow prepares consistent grayscale images and subtracts the training mean face. Principal component analysis then learns directions of variation, giving each image a smaller set of coefficients.

Find the nearest neighbour.
A test image is projected into the same learned space. A one-nearest-neighbour classifier assigns the identity of the closest training example. Reconstruction and recognition are checked separately.

A custom photo dataset.
I replaced the reference dataset with photographs of people around me. Recognition worked in this trial, with qualitatively similar results. A separate numerical evaluation of my custom dataset is still needed.

38 of 40. In the ORL report.
The original ORL report records thirty-eight correct predictions out of forty test images, or ninety-five percent. The matrix shown here is that reference result, not a measurement of my custom photographs.

A representation you can inspect.
The project connects preprocessing, dimensionality reduction, and classification. Next steps are to document the custom split, repeat evaluation, and test lighting changes. Read the full case study on my portfolio.
