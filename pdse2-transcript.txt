A closer look at PdSe₂.
How do edge chemistry and dispersion corrections change electronic predictions? My capstone investigates pentagonal palladium diselenide nanoribbons through density functional theory calculations.

Four edge families. Four modifications.
I modelled four hydrogen-passivated edge families: A A, Z Z, Z A, and S S. Fluorine and hydroxyl substitutions at central and lateral sites created four modified configurations.

Build. Relax. Compare.
Using Materials Studio and CASTEP, I optimised atomic structures and inspected band structures and density of states. The pristine comparison covers P B E, Grimme, and the T S correction.

A difference of six millielectronvolts.
For the pristine A A three example, the reported band gap is zero point five eight seven electronvolts with P B E, and zero point five eight one with Grimme.

A stronger method dependence.
Across the four modified structures, Grimme gaps range from zero point eight seven zero to zero point nine zero seven electronvolts. The corresponding P B E predictions are lower, as the thesis table shows.

Trace the prediction to its settings.
These reported calculations demonstrate sensitivity to the chosen method. They do not establish experimental accuracy. Explore the original models, plots, calculation settings, and limitations in the full project page.
