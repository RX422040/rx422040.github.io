COMPUTER VISION2026 · In progress

Vision II

Making sense of the penalty kick.

A reproducible, human-reviewed computer vision workflow for 39 World Cup penalties: kicking foot, run-up steps, goalkeeper behaviour, ball trajectories and observed outcomes.

39penalty kicks reviewed
4World Cup shoot-outs
PythonYOLO11OpenCVSciPy
Read the Vision II case studyWatch the project film
At a glance

Pretrained YOLO11 models provide visual observations. Project-specific rules extract features, optical flow compensates for camera motion, and motion-constrained beam search associates ball detections.

All 39 kicks have manually confirmed step counts. The automatic step baseline has an MAE of 2.66 steps across 35 successful runs. Goalkeeper and ball outputs retain review flags for uncertain cases.

The full case study includes methodology, evaluation, code attribution and an interactive explorer of all 39 penalties.

STATISTICAL MODELLING2025

Modelling fatigue life

Connecting process settings to performance.

Analysed 27 welding runs in R, comparing polynomial and log-transformed models to understand how current, voltage and electrode diameter relate to fatigue life.

MODEL-BASED INSIGHTConnect process settings to service life.
RResponse surfacesRegressionDiagnostics
Read the fatigue-life case study
Explore the approach

Fitted full quadratic models, examined residuals and selected a simpler log-scale model. Quantified an 8.68% fitted increase for a 0.1 increase in coded current, with a 95% interval of 7.70%–9.67%. The model predicts approximately 7,213 cycles at 300 A, 25 V and a 2.5 mm electrode within the studied range.

A closer look at PdSe₂

How dispersion corrections shape predictions.

Investigated the effect of van der Waals corrections on the electronic properties of pentagonal PdSe₂ nanoribbons through density functional theory simulations.

RESEARCH FOCUSFrom pristine edges to F / OH modifications.
DFTPBE / Grimme / TSBand structures
Read the PdSe₂ case studyWatch the short film
Explore the approach

Constructed four hydrogen-passivated edge families and four F/OH-modified configurations. Compared band structures and density of states: the pristine AA-3 PBE–Grimme gap difference is 6 meV, while the modified structures show larger method-dependent differences in the reported calculations.
